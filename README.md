# 🔐 Python Cybersecurity Projects

Hi there! I am Asmaul Hoque, a CSE 2nd year student at 
Government College of Engineering and Leather Technology. Salt Lake , Kolkata.
I am interested in Python and Cybersecurity. 
Here are my practice projects:

1. **Password Strength Checker** — A simple tool to check 
how strong your password is and whether it's safe from hackers.

2. **Login Log System** — Records login attempts with timestamps. 
A necessary technique to manage and monitor system security.

3. **Port Scanner** — Scans open ports on a system to identify 
vulnerabilities and keep your network safe.

4. **Caesar Cipher** — One of the oldest encryption methods. 
I built this to understand how secret messages are sent securely.

5. **Brute Force Simulator** — Demonstrates how attackers crack 
passwords by trying millions of combinations. Mine cracked 
"abc123" in 64 million attempts!
